# About ABP

ABP (Ananda Bazar Patrika) is one of the largest Bengali-language daily newspapers in India, headquartered in Kolkata, West Bengal. It was first published in 1922.

The ABP Group operates several media properties including ABP Ananda (Bengali news channel), ABP News (Hindi news channel), and ABP Live (digital news platform). The group covers news in multiple Indian languages and reaches readers across India and the global Bengali diaspora.

ABP Live publishes articles in English, Hindi, and Bengali on topics such as politics, business, entertainment, sports, technology, and lifestyle.
